# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds the project up:

- being kind and patient with questions, including repeated ones
- giving and accepting specific, actionable feedback
- assuming competence and good faith; asking before assuming intent
- focusing on what is best for the people who will run this in production

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- personal or political attacks, insults, or dismissive comments
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- public or private harassment, including sustained disruption of discussions
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and whenever someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may edit,
remove or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not comply. Responses escalate
from a private warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban, depending on severity and
repetition. Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face consequences
decided by the other maintainers.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
either through GitHub or by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/seradavid/gitlab-code-ownership-notifier/security/advisories/new)
if you would rather not use a public channel. All reports are reviewed and handled
confidentially; the reporter's privacy and safety come first.
